 *Huy Phan, Kieu Dang, Ojaswi Dulal et al. (Truman State University, University at Albany, State University of New York)*

 What’s most striking is how quickly watermark reliability collapses under routine transformations (paraphrase/translation) while the very fixes that harden robustness often impose unacceptable utility and governance costs.
 - LLM watermarking techniques cluster around deployment-critical choices—generation-time vs. training/post-processing embedding and public vs. private detection authority—where differing assumptions (logit access, sampling control, secret keys, model ownership) largely determine real-world feasibility and guarantees.
