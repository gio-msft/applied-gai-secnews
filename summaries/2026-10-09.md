 - Across 30 independently generated pipelines, the best reported weighted F1 reached 78.41±0.22% on UNSW-NB15 (XGBoost; also Random Forest 78.41±0.21%) and 76.38±0.99% on NSL-KDD (MLP), indicating modest run-to-run variability despite LLM-driven code synthesis.
 - Ablations show the RF–MI completion step is the largest contributor (dropping weighted F1 by 1.13 pp on UNSW-NB15 and 1.35 pp on NSL-KDD), while removing semantic components barely changes UNSW-NB15 (−0.08 pp) but substantially hurts NSL-KDD (−1.85 pp), implying dataset-dependent value of semantic feature lineage.
