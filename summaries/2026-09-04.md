 - AST verbosity creates a hard feasibility bottleneck: on the longer dataset, only 86/260 (33%) AST inputs fit at 4096 tokens and even at 16384 tokens only 222/260 (85%) fit, whereas both raw code and BTs maintained 260/260 (100%) coverage across all tested context sizes.
